01Your device

Revallis delivers a fifteen‑minute pulsed electromagnetic field session. You wear it, tap once, and it takes care of the rest.

The device is worn like a pair of glasses. A coil encircles the face, and a light tube running alongside it tells you what the device is doing, and it is the only display there is, and section 5 is the key to reading it.

There is no power switch. When nothing is running and nothing is plugged in, the device sleeps. Sleeping draws so little current that a full charge would hold for well over a year, so there is nothing an off switch would save. A press of the button wakes it.

Finding your serial number

Every device has a unique serial printed on its label, in the form A4F00F078856. The same number appears at the top of the Identity section on the app's Device screen. If you have more than one device, this is how you tell which is which, and it is the first thing to quote in any support request.

Evaluation units

These units run at a reduced output while the radio‑frequency stage is being finalised, so the magnetic field is below the published specification. Everything else, from timing and controls to light behaviour and the app, behaves exactly as the finished product will.

02Charging

Charge the device fully before first use. Connect a USB‑C cable to any standard charger or computer port.

The light gives one pulse the moment power is detected, and that pulse is your confirmation that the cable and charger are good. It then settles into a dim, slow breathing while charging, and goes dark when the battery is full. A full charge from empty takes about three to four hours.

Darkness therefore means one of two things: charged, or not connected. The acknowledging pulse is what separates them, so watch for it when you plug in.

You can run a session while charging. It works, but charging will slow or stop for the duration, because the available current goes to the device first.

Note

While plugged in, the device stays awake to drive the charging light, so the button responds instantly. On battery there is a brief moment while it wakes. See section 3.

03The button

Tap to start. Tap to pause. Hold until it flashes to stop. Keep holding for a second flash to pair.

That single sentence is the whole interface. What matters is that the flashes are the boundaries,you do not need to count seconds. Hold the button, watch the light, and release after the flash you want.

Each flash is a brief blackout followed by full brightness. A gap registers far more clearly than a brighten, particularly when the light is already lit.

Waking from sleep

To start, hold the button for about a second. The light comes on the moment it registers, so you do not need to let go first. A shorter press does nothing, which is what stops knocks in a bag or a car from starting sessions on their own. This is the same whether the device is asleep or already plugged in and charging.

Once a session is running, pausing, resuming and stopping are ordinary taps and holds. Only starting takes the deliberate press.

04A session

Put the device on, then tap. The light begins a slow two‑second breathing and the session runs for fifteen minutes. At the end the light fades gently and the device returns to idle, recording the session as completed.

You do not need to watch it. The light sits in peripheral vision while you read or work, and the breathing motion, rather than sheer brightness, is what tells you it is running.

Every session is recorded on the device, whether completed or interrupted, and can be read into the app later. Sessions are not lost if your phone is not present,the device keeps up to 64 of them and hands them over the next time you connect.

If it refuses to start

Three quick pulses, repeated three times, then darkness, means there is not enough charge to finish a full session. The device will not begin one it cannot complete. Put it on charge.

06The app

The app is optional. The device runs complete sessions on its own and keeps its own records. The app is for checking status, reading history, and updating firmware.

Pairing

  1. Hold the button until the light flashes a second time, at about seven seconds, then release. The light begins fast breathing.
  2. Open the app and tap Connect on the Device screen.

The pairing window stays open for two minutes, then closes on its own. After pairing once, the app reconnects by itself, so you should not need the button again.

One device, one phone

A device accepts one phone at a time. To move it to a different phone, put it back into pairing with the second flash; the new phone replaces the old one.

If you are setting up several devices, pair them one at a time. They all appear under the same name, so more than one in pairing mode at once means you cannot tell which you are connecting to.

07Firmware updates

Updates arrive as a single file. Save it somewhere your phone can reach it. A cloud folder or the Files app is fine. Then:

  1. Connect to the device and make sure no session is running.
  2. Go to Diagnostics and tap Update firmware.
  3. Choose the file. The transfer takes three to four minutes.

You can leave the app while it runs; the transfer continues in the background. When it finishes, the device restarts and checks the new firmware before running it. If that check fails, it returns to the previous version on its own, so an interrupted or faulty update cannot leave the device unusable.

After the restart, tap Reconnect. Confirm the new version number on the Device screen under Identity.

Note

Updating clears the device's event log. Export anything you need from Diagnostics first. Treatment history is not affected.

08Care and storage

Everyday

Wipe with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth. Do not immerse the device or use solvents. The device is not waterproof.

Storage

Charge before storing for long periods, and top it up every few months. Store away from direct sun and heat.

Do not open the device

There are no user‑serviceable parts inside, and the internal circuitry carries voltages well above the battery's. Opening the housing voids any warranty and risks damage to the device.

Do not use a device with a damaged housing, a swollen battery, or a cable or connector that has been damaged. Contact your supplier.
